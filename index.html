How it works

  1. Add media — drop pictures & videos into the library (nothing is uploaded; everything stays on your device).
  2. Pick a background — tap a video (or image) in the library. The first video you add is set automatically.
  3. Place pictures — tap a picture to put it on the canvas, then choose a frame: Polaroid, Stamp, Plain, Circle…
  4. Export — save the post as a PNG image or record it as a video. “All Posts → Video” joins every post into one clip.

Canvas controls

  • Drag a picture to move it
  • Corner handles or mouse wheel to resize · round top handle to rotate
  • On touch screens: pinch with two fingers to resize & rotate
  • Arrow keys nudge · Shift+arrows nudge faster · Del removes

Posting to Instagram

  • Canvas sizes match Instagram: 1:1 and 4:5 feed, 9:16 Reels/Stories
  • Chrome & Edge export MP4 directly — ready to post
  • Other browsers export WebM — convert to MP4 before posting
  • Video export records in real time; watch it live on the canvas
